- **A `not_applicable` applicability decision requires a rationale.** `DecisionInput` gains an if/then: when `decision` is `not_applicable`, `rationale` is required and must hold at least one character that is not whitespace, a control character or a zero-width/format character (the 2000-character cap is unchanged). `applicable` and `unresolved` keep it optional, and a `carry: true` item is not re-validated. The Backend refuses the violation with a closed 400 `rationale_required` (new closed body `RationaleRequiredError`, declared beside the shared error envelope on the write's 400, acknowledged in `scripts/breaking_schema_allowlist.json`).
- **Compliance document references: search by link substring (additive; contract only).** New operation `POST /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/agents/{agent_ref}/compliance-document-refs/search` (`project_compliance_document_refs.search_compliance_document_references`, `x-replay: read`) and closed request `DocumentReferenceSearchRequest` (`q` 1 to 200 characters after trimming, optional `include_withdrawn`, `page`, `per_page`). It is a read served as POST so the search text, which is customer content, stays out of URLs, logs and traces; it matches case-insensitively, as a literal substring, against the declared link and answers the existing `DocumentReferenceListResponse` with the list's ordering and paging.
